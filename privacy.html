Privacy Policy

Last modified: May 10, 2026

There are many different ways you can use our services — from getting a brand new website built for your business, to ongoing maintenance and design work. When you share information with us, we can provide you with a better, more personalized experience. We want to be clear about how we use your information and the ways you can protect your privacy.

Our Privacy Policy explains:

What's in this policy

  • What information we collect and why we collect it
  • How we use that information
  • When we share information
  • The choices we offer
  • How we keep your information safe
  • Changes to this policy
  • How to contact us

Information we collect

We collect information to provide better services to everyone we work with — from understanding what kind of website you need, to keeping you updated on your project's progress.

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you give us. When you reach out to us for a quote, fill out our contact form, or hire us to build your website, we ask for personal information like your name, email address, phone number, and business name. This helps us communicate with you and deliver your project.

Information we get from your use of our website. We collect information about how visitors interact with our website, including which pages are visited and how long visitors spend on each page. This information includes:

  • Usage data — details about how you navigated our site, such as pages visited and links clicked
  • Device information — browser type, operating system, and screen size so we can ensure our site works well for you
  • Location information — general location based on your IP address

Cookies and similar technologies. We use cookies to collect and store information when you visit our website. Cookies help us understand how our site is being used and improve the experience for future visitors.

How we use information we collect

We use the information we collect from our website and services to provide, maintain, and improve what we offer — and to communicate with you clearly throughout your project.

We will never use your information to send unsolicited marketing emails or share your data with advertisers.

Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Deliver the web design and development services you hired us for
  • Send you project updates, invoices, and important communications
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Understand how our website is being used so we can improve it
  • Comply with any legal obligations we may have

We use Google Analytics on our website to help us understand traffic patterns. Google Analytics collects usage data anonymously — it does not identify you personally.

Information we share

We do not share your personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Sleek Site unless one of the following circumstances applies:

With your consent. We will share personal information outside of Sleek Site when we have your permission to do so.

For legal reasons. We will share personal information if we have a good-faith belief that doing so is necessary to meet any applicable law, regulation, or enforceable legal request — or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sleek Site or our clients.

We may share non-personally identifiable information publicly — for example, general statistics about website traffic trends.

Transparency and your choices

We want you to feel in control of your information. Here is what you can do:

  • Opt out of communications. You can ask us to stop sending you emails at any time by replying to any email we send or contacting us directly.
  • Request your data. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you at any time.
  • Request deletion. You can ask us to delete any personal information we hold about you, and we will do so unless we are legally required to keep it.
  • Manage cookies. You can set your browser to block or delete cookies at any time. Note that some parts of our website may not work correctly without cookies enabled.

Information security

We work hard to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Specifically:

  • Our website uses SSL encryption to protect data transmitted between your browser and our site
  • We limit access to personal information to only those who need it to deliver your project
  • We regularly review our data practices to ensure your information stays safe

While we do our best to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We want to be transparent about that.

Children's privacy

We do not direct our services to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services grow and evolve. When we make changes we will post the updated policy on this page with a new modification date. We encourage you to check back periodically — especially if you are a returning client — so you always know how we are protecting your information.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please reach out to us. We are happy to help.
